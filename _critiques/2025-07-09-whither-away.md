The most important fact about “Whither Away” is visible only when the prompt is read. The original essay concerns voluntaryism, anarcho-capitalism, and whether government can be displaced by alternatives. Its preserved prompt concerns personal decisions, analysis paralysis, adventure, and a different proposed song. The essay is coherent enough on its own that the mismatch can pass unnoticed.

As a political piece, v1 has an effective image: a monster loses importance as people build something beyond its shadow. It also gives Contra room to ask who protects people when formal institutions withdraw. But both sides rely on sweeping claims about history and power. Government supposedly grows whenever challenged, voluntary systems always work despite it, and power supposedly never disperses. The metaphor supplies conclusions that the prose has not demonstrated.

Version 1.5 edits the artifact that exists. It separates voluntaryism from the particular property arrangements of anarcho-capitalism, removes claims that elections or protests necessarily strengthen the state, and asks how alternative institutions protect people with little bargaining power. Contra’s centralization argument is also narrowed: coordination is necessary in some tasks, but that does not establish a single central authority as the only answer. The malformed music link is repaired while the original song remains recognizable.

The decisive editorial choice is the explicit note identifying the prompt mismatch and linking to the fresh treatment. Silently turning this entry into an essay on personal choices would have made “v1.5” a misleading label. The political reading list is therefore retained with an explanation of what it does and does not answer.

Version 2 follows the actual prompt. Its map-covered table, fictional relocation example, and distinction between checkable uncertainty and experience-dependent uncertainty create a different essay. The strongest exchange tests both premature commitment and endless experimentation. A decision note with reasons, warning signs, and a reconsideration date gives “follow through” limits in both directions.

For fidelity to the recorded prompt, v2 clearly wins. For preserving and improving the original political essay, only v1.5 attempts that task. This is not clean evidence that newer generation produces a better argument on the same topic; the topics differ. The cause of the mismatch is not established by these files and should not be invented.

V2 still risks making difficult life choices sound more manageable than they are, especially when small trials are unavailable. Its acknowledgment of shared costs helps. The wider lesson is editorial: prompt fidelity requires checking the artifact against the prompt, not merely admiring a fluent response.
