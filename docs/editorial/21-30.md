# Editorial record: July 21–30

This record covers 10 edited v1.5 entries, 10 fresh v2 entries, and 10 individual comparisons. The original `_posts` files were read in full and not modified. The JSON companion contains concrete per-entry commit-message bullets.

Every v1.5 retains its original song, recording links, and prompt. Recordings are explicitly labeled as original v1 recordings. V2 songs are original lyrics, with no claim of a new audio recording and no reuse of the old audio links. Prompt text remains verbatim, including July 23’s originally unheaded list and July 30’s AI-authored Novix prompt. The added July 23 heading is outside the preserved original block.

## Checks and scope

- Verified the complete preserved prompt as an exact suffix of each of the 20 version files, not just the six field values.
- Verified all 30 expected entry identifiers, one excerpt marker per file, and three version links per critique using `relative_url`.
- V2 essays are approximately 1,060–1,150 words before their prompt blocks; comparisons approximately 450–510 words including their lead and navigation. They are substantial essays with individualized arguments, not outlines.
- Searched the owned files for temporary markers, the Novixm typo, and zero-width characters; removed an accidental zero-width character before “Consider” in the infinity essay.
- Recommendations use author/publisher or original-research links in v2 where available; existing relevant v1 affiliate links are retained in v1.5. No new affiliate URLs were invented. Link identity checks do not certify current retail availability or every affiliate redirect.
- Scientific and technical corrections rely on original papers, official software documentation, and author/publisher material. Normative conclusions and hypothetical scenarios are arguments, not claims that an experiment measured the proposed result.

## Candidates for the closing reviews

- **Largest v1.5 factual improvement: Surfing the Wave.** Bell assumptions, no signalling, measurement, and ultraviolet divergences required substantive corrections. This is the clearest example where editing must resist a premise in the original prompt.
- **Largest historical improvement: Great Man Theory.** Correcting the Einstein/Minkowski sequence matters more than polishing the symphony metaphor. The retained poetic shorthand is labeled.
- **Largest fairness improvement: Learning to Be Human.** The opponent now argues for knowledge, instruction, and equitable support; the new reading list includes a serious contrasting lens.
- **Strong v2 candidates: Private Property and Anarchy, Life Necessitates Evil, and Finite Existence.** The garden/lender separates institutional jobs, the storm kitchen makes inaction visible, and the geometric series demonstrates its key distinction instead of merely asserting it.
- **Relative v2 limitations: Great Man Theory, Surfing the Wave, and All My Passion.** The first is more a method-of-history essay than a documented history; the second is dense and mostly Bohmian despite fairly naming alternatives; the third assumes unusual control of time in its hobby example, although it recognizes that limitation.
- **Prefer v1.5 for some uses.** The individual critiques prefer v1.5 for the original concise blog purpose on July 21 and July 27. Later does not mean uniformly better.
- **Across the group, v2 has its own recurring formula.** A hypothetical setting, a stronger opposing case, source-aware caveats, and a practical synthesis can become predictable. The corpus should admit that gain in argumentative discipline can cost spontaneity and musical exuberance.
- **Unresolved evidence limits remain.** None of these essays measures its proposed maintenance exercise, establishes causal effects of school choice, proves an anarchic property system scales, or selects one quantum ontology by experiment. The final review should distinguish a better argument from new empirical knowledge.
